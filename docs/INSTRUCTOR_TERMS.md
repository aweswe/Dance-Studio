Part A — Trainer Rules & Obligations
A1. Onboarding Requirements
All new trainers must complete the following before Portal access is activated and classes commence:
A1.1 Professional Profile: A complete instructor profile must be created on the Rhythmzz website including bio, specialty areas, a video reel (reel can be done within a month), and class schedule.
A1.2 Official Gear: All trainers must wear official Rhythmzz Academy of Dance branded gear during every class without exception, at the studio, partner venues, workshops, or any external event conducted under the Rhythmzz name. This is a non-negotiable brand standard.
A1.3 Identity Verification: Trainers must submit a valid Aadhaar card and PAN card for identity verification and tax compliance before their first payout is processed.
A1.4 Trainer Agreement: A signed copy of this document must be returned to the Studio Manager before the trainer’s first class. Digital acceptance via the Portal is also valid.
A1.5 Background Verification: Rhythmzz Academy of Dance reserves the right to conduct a background verification check at its sole discretion in the interest of student safety and brand reputation. Refusal to cooperate may result in withdrawal of the engagement offer.
A2. Social Media & Brand Obligations
A2.1 Minimum Posting Requirement: All trainers are required to post a minimum of 2 posts or one reel per week on for the social media accounts related to their work at Rhythmzz. All posts must tag @rhythmzzdance.live
A2.2 Brand Standards: All public content in which a trainer identifies themselves as a Rhythmzz instructor must reflect positively on the academy. Content that is offensive, controversial, or inconsistent with the Rhythmzz brand may result in a formal warning or suspension.
A2.3 Rhythmzz Content: Trainers must not use Rhythmzz branded content, logos, or materials for personal promotion outside the academy without prior written consent from the Head.
A3. Attendance, Scheduling & Class Conduct
A3.1  Attendance Marking: Trainers must mark student attendance accurately on the Portal on the same day as each class. Retrospective changes require written approval from the Studio Manager.
A3.2  Own Attendance Log: Trainers must log their own class attendance and teaching records through the My Log section of the Portal on the day of each class.
A3.3 Punctuality: Trainers are expected to be present at the studio at least 10 minutes before the scheduled start of each class.
A3.4 Schedule Changes: Timing changes to assigned batches must be made through the Portal only. Any change affecting students requires a minimum of 24 hours’ advance notice communicated via the Portal announcement system.
A3.5 New Batches: The creation of new batches or cancellation of existing ones requires prior written approval from Nitish Kumar (Founder & Artistic Director) or the Studio Manager.
A3.6 Professional Conduct: Trainers are expected to maintain the highest standard of professionalism with all students, parents, and colleagues always, inside the studio and in all communications.
A4. Student Data & Confidentiality
A4.1 Confidentiality: All student information accessed through the Portal — names, contact details, attendance records, fee status — is strictly confidential and must not be shared, exported, or used for any purpose outside the trainer’s role at Rhythmzz.
A4.2 Personal Device Storage: Student data must not be stored on personal devices or shared via personal messaging applications without prior authorization from the Studio Manager.
A4.3 Media & Photography: Photos or videos of students — particularly minors — may not be published publicly without verified parental consent. All media must be uploaded through the Portal and is subject to review before going live.
A5. Intellectual Property
A5.1 Ownership: All choreography, syllabi, training materials, and content created during a trainer’s engagement with Rhythmzz Academy of Dance remain the intellectual property of the academy, unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.
A5.2 Restriction on External Use: Trainers may not reproduce, distribute, or commercially use Rhythmzz content, methods, or branded materials outside the academy without prior written consent from the Head.
Part B — Trainer Benefits & Pay Structure
B1. Revenue Share & Payout
B1.1 Revenue Split: Trainers are paid on a revenue-share model based on verified platform bookings. No fixed salary is applicable unless separately agreed in writing.
B1.2 Payout Cycle: Payouts are processed on the 10th of each month for the previous month’s confirmed and completed sessions. Disputed or flagged sessions are held pending resolution.
B1.3 Deductions: The following are deducted from gross payout before disbursement: applicable TDS as per Indian tax law, late cancellation penalties (Clause C3), and any outstanding dues owed to the academy.
B1.4 Tax Compliance: All trainers are required to provide their PAN card details. Rhythmzz Academy of Dance will deduct TDS in accordance with Section 194J or applicable provisions of the Income Tax Act, 1961. Trainers are responsible for their own GST registration if turnover thresholds are met.
B1.5 Payment Method: Payouts are made via bank transfer or UPI to the account registered at onboarding. It is the trainer’s responsibility to keep banking details current.

Part C — Policies & Enforcement
C1. Non-Solicitation
C1.1  Students: Trainers are strictly prohibited from approaching or facilitating any Rhythmzz student to book, attend, or pay for classes outside the platform — whether through personal channels, third-party apps, or direct arrangements. This applies during and after the trainer’s engagement with the academy.
C1.2  Partner Studios: Where Rhythmzz introduces a trainer to a partner studio, the trainer agrees not to enter into any independent commercial arrangement with that studio for a period of 12 months following the conclusion of their engagement with Rhythmzz.
C1.3  Monitoring: Rhythmzz reserves the right to conduct periodic spot-checks. Where a trainer’s platform bookings decline significantly while their social media shows active classes, a formal investigation will be initiated.
C2. Platform Integrity
C2.1  All Sessions on Platform: Every class conducted under the Rhythmzz name must be booked, logged, and verified through the platform before the session begins. Unscheduled sessions will not be compensated regardless of the circumstances.
C2.2  No Fraudulent Bookings: Trainers must not facilitate, encourage, or participate in the creation of fraudulent student accounts or bookings. Any such conduct will result in immediate termination and potential legal action.
C2.3  Account Security: Two-factor authentication is mandatory. Login credentials must not be shared with any other person. Simultaneous logins will trigger an immediate account security review.
C3. Cancellation Policy
C3.1  Short-Notice Cancellation: A trainer-initiated cancellation made less than 6 hours before a scheduled class will result in a penalty of ₹200, deducted automatically from the trainer’s next payout.
C3.2  Repeat Cancellations: 3 or more trainer-initiated cancellations within a single calendar month will result in temporary suspension of the trainer’s profile pending a review with the Head.
C3.3  Force Majeure: Medical emergencies and documented unforeseen circumstances may be reviewed at the sole discretion of the Head. The decision of the Head is final.
C4. Disciplinary Action & Termination
C4.1  Warnings: Breaches of these Terms will result in a written warning. Two warnings within a 3-month period may result in suspension or termination.
C4.2 Immediate Termination: The following breaches will result in immediate termination without warning: falsification of attendance records, breach of student data confidentiality, fraudulent bookings, or conduct that brings the academy into disrepute.
C4.3  Post-Termination: Upon conclusion of engagement, Portal access will be deactivated immediately. The trainer must not retain any downloaded student data or use any content created during their tenure without written permission.
